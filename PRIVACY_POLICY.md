Bytes Studios ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to developing safe, engaging, and high-quality mobile applications and games. This General Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games and applications published by Bytes Studios on Google Play and other app platforms, including but not limited to **DoodleNibi** and upcoming releases.

This policy outlines how we handle user privacy, our strict adherence to child-safety regulations, and your rights regarding data control.

---

### 1. Children’s Privacy & Families Policy Compliance (COPPA / GDPR-K)

A significant portion of Bytes Studios' catalog is created specifically for children and families. Protecting young players' privacy is our highest priority:

- **COPPA & Google Play Families Policy**: We strictly adhere to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), European General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K), and Google Play’s Families Policy requirements.
- **No Personal Data Collection from Children**: For all our child-directed games, we do **not** knowingly collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, or persistent device identifiers.
- **Safe Environment**: Our games for children do not contain behavioral tracking, social media integrations, open chat functions, or unauthorized third-party links.

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use

Bytes Studios adheres to a **data minimization** approach across all applications:

#### A. Information We Do NOT Collect
Unless explicitly stated for a specific connected service, our games:
- Do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, or physical address.
- Do **not** access your contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, or storage files outside the app's private sandbox.
- Do **not** track or collect precise GPS or coarse geolocation data.
- Do **not** profile or track users across third-party apps or websites.

#### B. Local On-Device Data Storage
Our applications and games predominantly store data locally on your device:
- **Game Progress & Preferences**: Save files, unlocked content, player achievements, and settings (such as sound and haptic vibration preferences) are stored strictly on your local device storage (e.g., standard Android `SharedPreferences` or local application storage).
- **Offline Playability**: For our offline titles, game state data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to remote servers.

---

### 3. Device Permissions

Our applications only request permissions that are strictly necessary to provide core game features:

- **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)**: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during interactive gameplay (can be enabled or disabled at any time in the app settings).
- **Network Permissions**: Many of our games are 100% offline and do not request the `INTERNET` permission. For games that do utilize network access (e.g., for optional online multiplayer or cloud backup), data transmission is strictly limited to authorized functional gameplay needs.

We never request intrusive or sensitive permissions such as Location, Read Contacts, or Storage Access outside the app sandbox.

---

### 4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

- **Ad-Free Games**: Games designated as ad-free (including our early-childhood games) contain **zero** third-party advertisements and include no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Child-Safe Ads (If Applicable)**: If any family-directed game features advertising, we exclusively partner with Google Play Families Self-Certified Ad SDKs. These deliver only contextual, child-safe advertisements without behavioral profiling or cross-site tracking.
- **In-App Purchases**: In-app purchases, where present in applicable titles, are handled entirely through official platform billing (such as Google Play In-App Billing). Bytes Studios never sees, collects, or stores your credit card or financial information.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

- Our standalone offline titles do **not** integrate third-party analytics, diagnostic trackers, or external marketing SDKs.
- If a future connected utility requires diagnostic tools (such as crash reporting), only anonymized, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry will be utilized, following strict data protection standards.

---

### 6. Data Control, Retention, and Deletion

Because progress and user-created content are saved locally on your device:

- **Complete User & Parent Control**: You have direct, unilateral control over all saved data at all times.
- **How to Delete Your Data**:
  1. Open your device's **Settings** menu.
  2. Navigate to **Apps** (or **Application Manager**) > select the relevant Bytes Studios app (e.g., *DoodleNibi*).
  3. Select **Storage & Cache** > tap **Clear Storage** (or **Clear Data**).
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling the application from your device will permanently erase all local data and saves.
- **Inquiries**: For any questions regarding data handling or data deletion assistance, parents and users can reach out to our contact email below.

---

### 7. Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Because our games do not transmit personal data over public networks and store game states within the protected Android application sandbox, your data is inherently protected against remote interception.
